# This si smy firts post for this app

Create wall framing plans with stud framer - the wall framing calculator that handles stud layout, cut lists, and blueprint generation so you can focus on building. Frame any wall type, see every stud position instantly, and export printable blueprints with a complete material list.

## Straight Wall Framing: Automatic Stud Layout & Cut List

Instant Stud Layout
Enter your wall length, height, and stud spacing and the wall framing calculator places every stud automatically - top plate, bottom plate, king studs, jack studs, cripples, and headers included.

Windows, Doors & Intersecting Walls
Add openings and intersections anywhere along the wall. The stud calculator recalculates framing around each element instantly, so you always have an accurate layout.

Complete Cut List
Generate a detailed cut list with quantity, size, and length for every component - ready to hand to your crew or take to the lumber yard.
